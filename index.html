Questions

What buyers ask first

Why is there no fee to start?

The factory pays me a commission on confirmed orders, so costing a style costs you nothing. The sample fee only covers the time spent chasing and checking samples.

Do you hold my money or the goods?

No. You pay the factory directly and the goods ship from the factory. I coordinate and report.

Can I talk to the factory myself?

Yes. You approve samples and confirm the order with the factory. I stay on the thread for follow-up.

What if a sample fails?

I report the measurement gaps with photos, agree the fix with the factory, and track the re-sample. You do not approve an order until the sample passes.
